# Cost Schedule — NIFTY Options

Primary sample: 2024-10-01 through 2026-04-30.

## Paytm Money
- F&O brokerage: Rs 10 per executed F&O order (current public F&O FAQ).
- Count each leg open/close and each roll open/close as a separate executed order.

## Statutory / exchange charges
- STT: 0.10% of option premium on option sells through 2026-03-31; 0.15% from 2026-04-01.
- NSE equity-option exchange/IPFT charge model: Rs 35.03 per Rs 1 lakh premium turnover per side through 2026-02-28; Rs 35.53 per Rs 1 lakh from 2026-03-01.
- SEBI turnover fee: 0.0001%.
- Stamp duty: 0.003% on option premium on buyer-side transactions.
- GST: 18% on brokerage plus exchange charges plus SEBI turnover fee.

## Slippage
Primary: 1.0 option point adverse slippage per option leg execution.
Sensitivity grid: 0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0 points.

Signal and stop calculations use observed market prices before costs. Execution costs affect realized P&L only.
